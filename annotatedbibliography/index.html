This article discusses the importance of green spaces in cities and the overall benefits. This article argues that green spaces should be taken more into consideration when making cities because they have numerous benefits, such as increasing individuals' life expectancy, reducing the heat island effect, and improving individuals' overall mental health. I found this article really insightful on the crucial role green spaces play in cities. I now know that green spaces are not just ornamental pieces in cities but actually serve a greater purpose.

After reading this article, I now have a greater understanding of the impact trees have on cities. They help promote biodiversity, reduce heat, and are beneficial to individuals' health. Different types of trees and plants promote biodiversity in cities, which is beneficial for preventing and reducing the harm of natural disasters. Trees cool down cities by absorbing carbon dioxide, and soil stores carbon and water, which prevents flooding from occurring. Green spaces in general are beneficial for people’s health by promoting outside movement and giving people a space to relieve their stress.
