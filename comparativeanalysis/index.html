Comparative Analysis

The first website I chose to compare my project to is the Google Maps 360 feature on Google Maps, because a part of my project is guiding users through a fully green city. I like that this website lets users navigate through a location as if they were walking through that area. I want my project to have this effect, but also look more gamified and less hyper-real.

The other website I chose to compare my project with is an architecture firm’s projects page. I am comparing this to my project because I also want to add architectural illustrations to give people a better idea of how the city would function. In my project, I want simple yet effective drawings that give people a clear sense of what it would feel like to live in this city.
